From a corner griddle to four neighborhood kitchens

In 2021, two friends set up a single flat-top in a Fishtown pop-up with a simple idea — press a ball of fresh beef hard onto a screaming-hot griddle, let the edges lace and caramelize, and serve it before it ever had a chance to cool down. The line went around the block by week two.

Four neighborhood locations later, nothing about the craft has changed. We still hand-form every patty, still smash to order, and still make our OK sauce in-house. What's new is the tech that gets it to you faster: live tracking, easy reorders and rewards on every bite.

  • Never-frozen beef, delivered daily
  • Griddle-smashed to order, every time
  • OK sauce made fresh in every kitchen
