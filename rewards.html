Rewards FAQ

You earn 10 points for every $1 spent — automatically applied when you're signed in. Bonus points come from reviews, referrals, birthdays and installing the app.

Points stay active as long as you place at least one order every 12 months. After 12 months of inactivity, unused points expire.

Open the Redeem tab, choose a reward you can afford, and add it to your cart. The points are deducted at checkout.

Most reward redemptions can be combined with everyday menu pricing, but not with other promo codes on the same item. See full terms for details.
